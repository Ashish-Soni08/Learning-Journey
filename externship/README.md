# Wayfair AI Agent Engineering for Business Intelligence Externship

## [Externship Overview](https://app.extern.com/home)

This repository documents my work in a hands-on externship focused on building AI agents for business intelligence in furniture e-commerce.

In this externship, I am using tools like **n8n** and **Gemini** to build AI workflows that:

- track design and product trends,
- monitor competitor activity,
- generate content and marketing insights,
- and power a live dashboard for decision-making.

The goal is to show how modern supply chain and vendor teams can use AI agents to move from raw market signals to action faster.

## Know the Host Company

Wayfair makes it easy for millions of people to design spaces that feel like home. Its marketplace spans furniture, lighting, rugs, kitchenware, and decor, connecting shoppers to over 30 million products from 20,000+ global suppliers.

At a glance:

- ~$12B annual revenue
- 21M active customers
- 12,000+ employees across North America and Europe

Wayfair is one of the largest online home retailers and remains founder-led more than two decades after launch.

## Business Challenge

My mission in this externship is to build AI agents for Wayfair's **Rugs** category that can:

- track design trends,
- monitor competitor activity,
- and generate marketing-ready content ideas.

In a market where customer taste and competitor behavior change quickly, the advantage comes from spotting meaningful patterns early and acting on them.

## What I Am Building

### 1) Moodboard Agent

An AI workflow that converts style prompts into curated visual outputs for trend exploration and creative direction.

### 2) Market Trend Discovery Agents

Automated agents that collect and analyze data from product listings, blogs, and social channels to identify emerging design trends.

### 3) Competitor Monitoring Agents

Pipelines that track competitor launches, pricing changes, and campaign activity to surface market movements.

### 4) AI Insight Generation

Agents that transform raw trend and competitor signals into:

- concise summaries,
- draft blog/content ideas,
- and campaign caption suggestions.

### 5) Live Dashboard

An auto-updating Google Sheets dashboard combining trend, competitor, and AI-generated insights for stakeholder visibility.

## Project Themes

The externship is structured into five connected projects:

1. Creating a Moodboard Agent that connects design ideas to visuals
2. Discovering market demand and trends across products, blogs, and social media
3. Benchmarking competitors to find whitespace opportunities
4. Generating AI-driven insights and marketing content ideas
5. Building a live Market Intelligence Dashboard that brings it all together
